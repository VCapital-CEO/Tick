Trading is risky

Cryptocurrency prices are volatile. You can lose some or all of the money you allocate to a bot. Only use money you can afford to lose.

Past performance

Verified results come from a paper-trading simulation and from historical backtests. Real trading differs: orders may fill at worse prices, fees differ, markets can be illiquid and exchanges can fail. Past results do not predict future returns.

Bots can fail

A bot can contain bugs, stop working or behave differently when markets change. A verification badge means a bot passed a limited test, not that it is safe or profitable.

Your keys

Use an exchange key with trading enabled and withdrawals disabled. Relay never needs permission to move money out of your account.
